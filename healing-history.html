Healing history, opening possibility.

We help communities process the histories they carry so old fear does not decide the future.

A room for truth

The past deserves care, not silence.

Our workshops in Warsaw, Bonn, Brussels, Budapest, and beyond create space for communities to name inherited pain, listen across difference, and interrupt the repetition of history.

We train local facilitators so the work can continue long after a workshop ends.
