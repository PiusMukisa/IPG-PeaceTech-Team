Pop-up clinics, wherever needed.

Short, focused clinics that bring Process Work into communities, gatherings, and organisations for a day or a weekend.

What is a pop-up?

Bringing the work to you.

A pop-up clinic is a mobile version of our practice. We arrive with a small team of facilitators and offer sessions, group process, and short teaching wherever people are already gathering.

Pop-ups are ideal for conferences, community centres, festivals, and organisations who want a taste of the work before committing to a longer programme.
