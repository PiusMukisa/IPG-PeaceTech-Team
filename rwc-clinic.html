Welcome to the clinic

Counseling rooted in access, dignity, and care.

River’s Way Community Clinic is a sliding-scale, affordable counseling clinic serving the greater Portland community — and providing intensive training for interns specializing in counseling skills.

The clinic provides internships for students at the Process Work Institute (PWI) of Portland, a non-profit devoted to research and training in counseling and conflict-resolution skills. We also welcome interns from Goddard, Antioch, Pacifica, Palo Alto, and many other universities.

All interns are supervised by licensed clinical practitioners (MSWs, PhDs, and PsyDs), and all services are confidential.

We offer services to people regardless of age, race, cultural background, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, or physical and mental abilities — anyone looking for support to resolve conflict, find meaning, or explore the feelings and challenges that make life difficult.
