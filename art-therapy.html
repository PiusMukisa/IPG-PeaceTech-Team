Art Therapy Program.

A training in using art — image, colour, movement, and making — as a way into healing, expression, and change.

01

The language of image

Learn to follow what a picture is trying to say before words arrive.

02

Materials as method

Paint, clay, collage, and movement as doorways into inner experience.

03

Holding a session

Facilitate art-making safely for individuals and groups.

04

Integration

Bring insight from the studio back into everyday life and relationship.

On the programme

Make first, understand after.

The Art Therapy Program blends studio practice, theory, and personal process. No previous art experience is needed — only willingness to explore.

Sessions run across the year with an option to continue toward the diploma. Places are limited so early registration is encouraged.
