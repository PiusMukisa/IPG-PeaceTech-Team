Peace with power & purpose

Building peace through human connection.

We help people cross lines of faith, identity, and history — and find new ways of living together.

Hey, we’re the International Peace Group.

Peacebuilders. Human rights advocates. Community partners.

We believe the power of relationship transforms lives. For more than two decades we have brought people together across the deepest lines of conflict — because change begins the moment two “enemies” choose to understand one another.

That’s where our work begins. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we help people and communities:

  • Find real alternatives to war and violence
  • Heal the history that keeps repeating itself
  • Build bridges across faith, culture, and identity
  • Stand for human rights, dignity, and equality

We know firsthand how hard peace can be

The work is relationship.

We know the frustration of conflicts that feel too old, too complex, or too painful to touch. We have sat in those rooms — and watched people who were once “the enemy” discover they are not so different after all.

That’s why we are passionate about connecting communities through honest conversation, conflict resolution, and the slow, brave work of healing history. Showing up authentically helps reduce fear, rebuild trust, and create a more peaceful world.

Our values

Heart & Soul

We truly care about the people and communities we serve. We put our whole hearts into every relationship and every place we work.

Courage

We move toward conflict with curiosity, not fear. We are not afraid of hard conversations or bold, unfamiliar ideas.

Compassion

When people show up as their true selves, understanding follows. We honor every story and every side with care and respect.

Justice

Behind everything we do is intention. We stand for human rights, equality, and dignity — for everyone, everywhere.

We believe in a more human future for all.

We believe in inclusion, equality, unity, freedom, and empowerment — living fully in the moment and doing the work that builds lasting peace.

03Board of directors

The people who hold the work.

Our board brings decades of practice across conflict transformation, human rights, therapy, education, and community organizing.

GR

Dr. Gary Reiss

Director

Dr. Gary Reiss holds an LCSW, PhD., and is a certified trainer in Process-oriented Psychology. Gary has a private practice in Eugene and Portland, Oregon, and teaches Process oriented Psychology worldwide, now actively teaching in 17 countries.

He is a senior faculty member at the Process Work Institute, and brings 40 plus years’ experience to his work. His specialties include family therapy, sex therapy, working with coma patients, Worldwork in hot spots in the world, organizational development, and integrating Process work with different spiritual traditions.

He has published 10 books including The Dance of Sex, Dreaming Money, and Families that Dream Together. His latest book is Love, Power, and Wisdom. He is the director of the International Peace Group.

IH

Ibrahim Hamide

President

Ibrahim Hamide is a long-time activist in the Eugene community. He is a member of the Eugene Human Rights Commission, past president of the Eugene Middle East Peace Group, and has been given many awards by the city of Eugene for his human rights efforts.

SE

Sage Emery

Vice-President and Secretary

Sage Emery is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist and Certified in Second Training through Arny and Amy Mindell. She has been in private practice for over 20 years and has taught internationally in over 10 countries with her partner Gary Reiss and on her own.

Sage’s international teaching specializes in women’s empowerment work. Sage is one of the key international organizers supporting the Magarini Center in Kenya Africa.
