Peace grows where people feel seen.

We create the conditions for people and communities to meet across difference, repair what has been broken, and imagine what comes next.

A practice, not a slogan

Connection is where change begins.

The International Peace Group is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit whose purpose is to support alternatives to war, the prevention of war, and basic principles of human rights worldwide.

Our work is grounded in the belief that no conflict is too complex for people to approach with curiosity, courage, and care.
