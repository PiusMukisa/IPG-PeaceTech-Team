Program description.

The full picture — how the programme is structured, what you will study, and how the diploma comes together.

Structure

How the programme is built.

The programme is organised into modules that can be taken in sequence or as standalone trainings. Each module combines taught material, supervised practice, and personal process.

Alongside the modules you build a portfolio of practice hours and reflective work. Together these form the basis of the diploma.

Faculty are experienced practitioners who teach internationally. You are supported one-to-one throughout, not left to find your own way.
